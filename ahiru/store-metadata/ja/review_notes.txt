[About the app]
An exam-preparation app for Japanese students (elementary school students preparing for junior-high entrance exams, and junior-high students preparing for high-school entrance exams): a textbook with animated diagrams, a formula collection, about 16,000 practice questions in five subjects and school-specific courses. Japanese only. Not affiliated with any school (stated in the description).

[Sign-in]
Most content works without an account. Sign-in is required only before buying a one-time (consumable) item, so the unlocked content is saved to the user's account. Buying a PRO/MAX subscription does not require sign-in. To test the one-time items, please sign in with the demo account in the App Review Information section.

[What is new in 2.2.10]
- "Textbook for school exams" (定期テストの教科書) for junior-high students. Where: Home -> "テスト対策" (Test prep) -> tap "定期テストの教科書（中1〜中3）". Pick a grade (中1/中2/中3) and a subject, then open a unit: an animated explanation (12+ slides), worked examples and check questions. Free users can read the first 2 slides and the first section of each unit; the rest is part of the existing PRO/MAX subscriptions (no new product).
- "やさしい問題から" (start from easy questions): on the same screen, tap the "やさしい問題から 50問" card. 50 basic questions per grade and subject, in three steps (easy, normal, test-like). Free users can try the first 5 questions of step 1; the rest is part of PRO/MAX.
- "おさらい" (review of earlier lessons) for all subjects: a collapsible box at the top of each textbook unit and the slide "つまずいたら、ここ" in answer explanations. Plain educational text, free.
- A one-tap survey asking why a subscription ended. It appears on the Home screen only for users whose subscription has ended or expired (e.g. a sandbox tester after the sandbox subscription expired) and stores the chosen reason without personal information.
- No new permissions, SDKs or in-app purchase products. Prices are unchanged.

[Subscriptions] (auto-renewable, monthly)
- PRO: 1,980 JPY / month. MAX: 2,890 JPY / month (PRO plus the AI weakness coach, English vocabulary Pro and Eiken practice).
An introductory offer (pay up front, 500 JPY for the first month) is configured for both plans. It is shown on the subscription screen to eligible new subscribers, once per Apple Account; afterwards the regular price renews automatically.
Where to find them: tap any locked item (e.g. a locked lesson in the Textbook tab) or the upgrade banner. The subscription screen shows both plans, prices, billing period, auto-renewal terms, "Restore Purchases", and links to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. All purchases on iOS use Apple's in-app purchase; the app does not link to any outside payment method.

[One-time items] (consumables, not subscriptions)
Formula unlock 200 JPY and formula bundle for one subject 2,980 JPY: Home -> choose an exam type -> 公式 (Formula) tab -> pick a subject -> tap a locked card. Single lesson unlock 150 JPY: Home -> 中学受験 -> 教科書 (Textbook) -> 算数 -> grade 4 lesson "わり算のあまりの活用" -> "¥150で解放する". Screenshots are attached to each in-app purchase page.

[AI features] (MAX plan)
The AI tutor and the English conversation practice send the text the user types to our server, which asks an AI model for an answer. The user is not asked for personal information.

[User-generated content]
No user-to-user chat and no user-generated content shown to others. Users can send private feedback to the developer through a form. The ranking shows only the user's own rank and score. Practice questions are original, tendency-based questions (not actual past exam papers).

[Account deletion]
Progress tab > scroll to the bottom > "アカウントを削除" (Delete account) deletes the account and its data.
