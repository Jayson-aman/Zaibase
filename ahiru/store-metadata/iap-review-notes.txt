==================================================
1) 公式集解放  com.zaibase.exam.formulaunlock  （消耗型・¥200）
==================================================
This is a consumable in-app purchase that unlocks ONE locked "formula card" (公式集) in our exam-preparation app. It is not a subscription and does not renew.

Context: in the Formula tab, each subject and exam type shows its first 8 formula cards for free. The other cards are locked and can be unlocked one at a time with this item (200 JPY each). Each purchase unlocks one card permanently. When a user has bought 15 single cards in one subject, the remaining locked cards of that subject are unlocked automatically, so the cost per subject is capped.

How to find it:
Sign in with the demo account (App Review Information) -> Home -> choose an exam type (中学受験 or 高校受験) -> 公式 (Formula) tab at the bottom -> pick a subject (e.g. 算数) -> scroll past the free cards -> in a locked card, tap "この1項目だけ ¥200 で解放" (unlock this one item for 200 JPY).

The purchase is tied to the user's account: sign-in is required before buying, so the unlocked card is saved to the account and survives reinstalling. The attached screenshot shows the locked card with the 200 JPY button.

==================================================
2) 公式集まとめ買い  com.zaibase.exam.formulabundle  （消耗型・¥2,980）
==================================================
This is a consumable in-app purchase that unlocks ALL currently locked formula cards of one subject within one exam type (for example, all locked cards of 中学受験 / 算数) with one purchase. It is not a subscription and does not renew. It is a discounted alternative to buying the cards one by one at 200 JPY.

How to find it:
Sign in with the demo account -> Home -> choose an exam type -> 公式 (Formula) tab -> pick a subject -> in any locked card, tap "ぜんぶ ¥2,980 で解放する" (unlock all for 2,980 JPY). The purchase applies to the subject and exam type shown on the screen.

The unlocked cards are saved to the user's account. The attached screenshot shows the bundle button (the same screen also shows the single-card button of the other product).

==================================================
3) 単元解放  com.zaibase.exam.unitunlock  （消耗型・¥150）
==================================================
This is a consumable in-app purchase that unlocks ONE additional textbook lesson (単元). It is not a subscription and does not renew.

Context: the textbook has about 5,800 lessons. In each subject and exam type, the first 15 lessons are free, and the other lessons are included in the PRO/MAX subscriptions. In addition, a set of extra lessons for each grade and subject (for example, grade 4 math) can be bought one at a time with this item, for users who do not want a subscription: the first 5 extra lessons of each grade and subject are free, and the others are locked. The top of a locked lesson can still be previewed. Each purchase unlocks one lesson permanently.

How to find it (example lesson):
Sign in with the demo account -> Home -> choose 中学受験 -> 教科書 (Textbook) tab -> 算数 -> open the grade 4 lesson "わり算のあまりの活用(あまりの処理を判断する文章題)". It is locked, and the lesson page shows the "¥150で解放する" button at the bottom. (Another example: "和差算の基礎(2つの数の和と差から求める)".) Locked lessons that are not in this extra set open the PRO/MAX subscription screen instead.

Sign-in is required before buying, and the unlocked lesson is saved to the user's account. The attached screenshot shows the locked lesson with the 150 JPY button.
